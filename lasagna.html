Description

This recipe on a creamy, packed of flavor lasagna will wow you on the first bite!

Ingredients

For 12 servings:

  • 1 pound lean ground beaf
  • 32 ounces of spaghetti sauce
  • 32 ounces of cottage cheese
  • 3 cups of shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of grated Parmesan cheese
  • salt
  • ground black pepper
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1/2 cup of water
